\begin{abstract}
We compare Einstein--Cartan actions with LC-Weyl, irreducible EC-Weyl,
and Ricci-tracefree EC quadratic invariants on compact homogeneous spatial
geometries times a circle. At fixed geometry, circle-dependent axial and
vector-trace torsion leaves irreducible EC-Weyl equal to LC-Weyl; the additional
nine-component and four-form sectors supply the axial gradient term.
For the Ricci-tracefree action we classify all smooth periodic stationary
torsion profiles: nonpositive coupling admits only zero torsion, while
positive coupling admits zero,
resonant harmonic, and constant mixed branches, all restricted saddles.
Constant relative torsion-axis orientations have extrema and degeneracies
determined by spatial anisotropy. For a coupled periodic shape--twist family
on $S^3\times S^1$, the nine-component sector contributes a further quadratic
frequency term, while all three actions share a strictly positive quartic
curvature coefficient. At fixed amplitude their high-frequency actions grow
for negative coupling and decrease without bound for positive coupling;
finite-frequency configurations below the round reference can remain.
Five normalized static shape directions exhibit the nonstationary Nil
reference and its flat paths, whose fixed compact quotients can be distinguished
by an integral harmonic-form invariant. These are results for specified
Euclidean variation families, with the static identities and classifications
of the preceding study used as reference results.
\end{abstract}
